# Supplementary FAQ (served via MCP Filesystem Server)

This file lives outside the main knowledge base vector store and is intentionally exposed only
through the MCP Filesystem Server, to demonstrate the Retriever Agent pulling live context from
an external system rather than only from the vector database.

## Hybrid / Remote Work
As of the latest company-wide update, employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week without
prior approval. Fully remote arrangements beyond 2 days/week require VP approval and are reviewed
every 6 months.

## New Joiner Laptop Delay
If your laptop has not arrived by Day 3, escalate directly through the #onboarding-escalations
Slack channel in addition to your Service Desk ticket — this is the fastest path during the first week.

## Emergency HR Contact
For urgent HR matters outside business hours (e.g., bereavement leave, safety concerns), contact
the 24/7 HR emergency line published on the intranet homepage, rather than waiting for a portal response.
