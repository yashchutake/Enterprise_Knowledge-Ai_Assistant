# Leave & Time-Off Policy

## Overview
This document describes the leave entitlements available to all full-time employees of the company. Leave is accrued monthly and can be viewed at any time in the HR portal.

## Casual Leave
Every full-time employee is entitled to 12 casual leaves per calendar year, accrued at the rate of 1 day per month. Casual leave can be used for short personal matters and does not require supporting documents. Unused casual leave lapses at the end of the calendar year and cannot be carried forward.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 sick leaves per calendar year. Sick leave beyond 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate to be uploaded to the HR portal within 5 working days of returning to work. Sick leave cannot be encashed.

## Earned / Privilege Leave
Employees accrue 1.5 days of earned leave per month, totaling 18 days per year. Earned leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 45 days. Any balance beyond 45 days will be automatically encashed at the end of the financial year. Earned leave requires manager approval at least 3 working days in advance, except in emergencies.

## Maternity and Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is granted for 26 weeks as per statutory requirements. Paternity leave is granted for 2 weeks and must be availed within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.

## Bereavement Leave
Employees are entitled to 5 days of paid bereavement leave in the event of the death of an immediate family member (spouse, parent, child, or sibling).

## Leave Application Process
1. Log into the HR portal and navigate to "Apply Leave".
2. Select the leave type, start date, end date, and reason.
3. Submit the request; it routes automatically to your reporting manager.
4. You will receive an email notification once the leave is approved or rejected.

## Unpaid Leave
Employees who have exhausted all paid leave categories may apply for unpaid leave of absence, subject to approval from their manager and HR Business Partner. Unpaid leave beyond 30 days may affect benefits continuity and requires VP-level sign-off.

## Public Holidays
The company observes 10 public holidays per year, published in the HR portal calendar at the start of each fiscal year. Employees in client-facing roles may be asked to work on a public holiday with a compensatory day off granted within the following 30 days.
