# IT Support & Asset Policy

## Overview
This document explains how employees can request IT support, what equipment is provided, and the rules for acceptable use of company IT assets.

## Raising a Support Ticket
All IT issues must be logged as a ticket through the internal Service Desk (accessible at helpdesk.company.internal). Do not email IT staff directly for routine issues, as this bypasses SLA tracking. Tickets are automatically prioritized as follows:
- **P1 (Critical):** Complete inability to work, production outage. Response time: 30 minutes.
- **P2 (High):** Major feature broken, no workaround. Response time: 4 hours.
- **P3 (Medium):** Minor issue with a workaround available. Response time: 1 business day.
- **P4 (Low):** Cosmetic issue or feature request. Response time: 3 business days.

## Laptop and Hardware
New employees are issued a standard-issue laptop within 3 working days of their start date. Laptop refresh cycles occur every 3 years. Employees who need specialized hardware (e.g., additional monitors, graphics workstations) must raise a request through their manager, who will approve based on role requirements.

## Password and Account Policy
Passwords must be at least 12 characters, include a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols, and must be rotated every 90 days. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all corporate accounts, including email, VPN, and internal applications. Accounts are automatically locked after 5 failed login attempts and can be unlocked via the Service Desk after identity verification.

## VPN and Remote Access
All employees accessing internal systems from outside the office network must connect via the corporate VPN client. VPN access is automatically provisioned on Day 1. VPN sessions time out after 8 hours of inactivity and must be re-authenticated.

## Software Installation
Employees may install software from the approved Software Center without additional approval. Any software outside the approved catalog requires a business justification submitted through the Service Desk and review by the Security team, typically completed within 5 business days.

## Data Backup
Company laptops back up automatically to cloud storage every 24 hours when connected to Wi-Fi. Employees should not store business-critical data solely on local drives.

## Equipment Return
All company-issued IT assets must be returned within 5 working days of an employee's last working day, coordinated through the exit checklist in the HR portal.
