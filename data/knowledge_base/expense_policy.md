# Travel & Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Overview
This document explains how employees can claim reimbursement for business-related travel and expenses.

## Eligible Expenses
The following categories are eligible for reimbursement when incurred for business purposes:
- Domestic and international travel (flights, trains, cabs)
- Hotel accommodation, up to the per-city cap defined in the Travel Grade table
- Client meals, up to $75 per person per meal
- Internet and mobile reimbursement for approved work-from-home setups, up to $30/month
- Conference and training fees pre-approved by the manager

## Submission Process
1. Log expenses in the Expense Portal within 15 calendar days of the expense being incurred.
2. Attach itemized receipts for any expense above $25. Expenses without receipts above this threshold will not be reimbursed.
3. Submit the expense report for manager approval.
4. Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle, typically within 10 business days.

## Travel Booking
All flights and hotels must be booked through the approved corporate travel portal to ensure duty-of-care tracking. Employees may book outside the portal only with prior written approval from their manager, and reimbursement will be capped at the equivalent corporate-portal rate.

## Per Diem
Employees traveling internationally receive a daily per diem to cover incidental expenses, with rates varying by destination country as published in the Travel Grade table. Per diem does not require itemized receipts.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses
The following are explicitly not reimbursable: alcohol (except at approved client entertainment events with prior approval), traffic fines, personal entertainment, and upgrades to business or first class without VP approval.

## Corporate Credit Card
Employees who travel frequently (more than 4 trips per quarter) are eligible for a corporate credit card. Corporate card statements must be reconciled monthly in the Expense Portal; unreconciled balances beyond 60 days may result in card suspension.
