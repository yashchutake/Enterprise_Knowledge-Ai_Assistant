# Information Security Policy

## Overview
This document outlines the mandatory security practices for all employees, contractors, and vendors accessing company systems and data.

## Data Classification
All company data must be classified into one of four tiers:
- **Public:** No restriction on sharing.
- **Internal:** Shared only within the company.
- **Confidential:** Shared only with individuals who have a defined business need.
- **Restricted:** Highly sensitive data (e.g., customer PII, financial records) requiring encryption at rest and in transit, and access limited to named individuals with explicit approval.

## Acceptable Use
Company systems must only be used for business purposes. Employees must not use company email or storage for personal file sharing of non-work-related large media files. Any suspected phishing email must be reported using the "Report Phishing" button in the email client, not simply deleted.

## Incident Reporting
Any suspected security incident — including a lost device, suspicious login, malware alert, or data leak — must be reported to the Security Operations Center (SOC) within 1 hour of discovery via the emergency hotline or the #security-incidents Slack channel. Delayed reporting beyond 24 hours may result in disciplinary action.

## Third-Party and Vendor Access
Any third-party vendor requiring access to internal systems must undergo a security review and sign a Data Processing Agreement before access is provisioned. Vendor access is reviewed quarterly and automatically revoked if unused for 60 consecutive days.

## Device Security
All company laptops must have disk encryption enabled, endpoint detection and response (EDR) software installed, and automatic OS updates turned on. Personal (BYOD) devices used to access company email must be enrolled in the Mobile Device Management (MDM) system.

## Remote Work Security
Employees working from public Wi-Fi (cafes, airports) must always connect through the corporate VPN and must never leave a company device unattended in a public place. Screens must be locked whenever a device is left unattended, even for a short period.

## Security Training
All employees must complete mandatory security awareness training within 30 days of joining and annually thereafter. Failure to complete training may result in suspension of system access until completed.
